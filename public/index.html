Frequently Asked Questions
Click the Deposit button in the sidebar or top bar, choose a method (M-Pesa, bank transfer, card, or crypto), enter an amount of at least $10, and confirm. Your balance updates instantly in this demo environment.
Open Withdraw from the sidebar, enter the amount and destination, and submit. Withdrawals are placed in a pending state and reviewed before funds are released, the same way a real broker would process a payout.
Forex trades let you buy or sell a currency pair with leverage and stay open until you close them, with profit or loss moving in line with the live price. Binary Options are a fixed stake on whether a price will be higher or lower after a set expiry time, with a fixed payout if you're right.
Leverage lets you control a larger position with a smaller amount of margin — for example, 1:50 leverage means $50 of exposure for every $1 of margin used. It magnifies both gains and losses, so higher leverage carries higher risk.
New accounts start in Demo Mode with virtual funds so you can practice risk-free. Your account mode is shown on the Settings page. Switching to live trading with real funds requires identity verification and is subject to local regulations.
Go to the sign-in page and select Password recovery. Enter your registered email, then follow the verification code prompt to set a new password.
Withdrawals are reviewed before release and can take some time depending on the payout method. You can track the status of any request under History. If it's been pending longer than expected, reach out using the contact details below.
Passwords are never stored in plain text, sessions are tied to your account only, and our support team will never ask for your password. Always verify you're on the official site before entering your credentials.
